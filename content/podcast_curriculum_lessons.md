### \[A1\_POD01\] The Morning Cafe (Sabah Kafesi & Sipariş Verme)

- **Süre:** 2:15 Dk (135 sn)  
- **Karakterler:** Liam (Barista \- Green Bean Cafe) & Emma (Müşteri / Öğrenci) \+ Host (Sunucu)  
- **İçerik:** Güneşli bir sabah kafesinde büyük boy yulaf sütlü latte ve ılık kruvasan siparişi verme, fiyat sorma, temassız kartla ödeme yapma ve Wi-Fi şifresi sorma.  
- **Hedef Dil Yapıları:** Nezaket kalıpları (Could I please have...), Fiyat sorma (How much is...?), 'To be' fiili, İşaret zamirleri (this/that).  
- **Anahtar Kelimeler:** oat milk (yulaf sütü), warm up (ısıtmak), total (toplam), receipt (fiş), password (şifre).

#### Tam Podcast Senaryosu (Spoken Script):

**\[Host Intro\]** Welcome to English Bites A1\! Today, Emma is ordering breakfast at the Green Bean Cafe. Listen to how she orders politely and pays for her food.

**Liam (Barista):** Good morning\! Welcome to Green Bean Cafe. What can I get for you today? **Emma:** Good morning\! Could I please have a large latte with oat milk? **Liam:** Sure\! Hot or iced? **Emma:** Hot, please. And could you warm up one of those chocolate croissants? **Liam:** Absolutely. Anything else for you today? **Emma:** No, that is all. How much is the total? **Liam:** That is six dollars and fifty cents. How would you like to pay? **Emma:** By card, please. Here is my contactless card. **Liam:** Perfect, it went through\! Here is your receipt. Your drink will be ready at the counter in two minutes. **Emma:** Thank you so much\! By the way, what is the Wi-Fi password? **Liam:** It is 'greenbean2026', all lowercase. **Emma:** Wonderful\! Have a great day\! **Liam:** You too\! Enjoy your coffee\!

**\[Host Outro\]** Great job\! Notice how Emma used "Could I please have..." to order politely. Now practice saying: "Could I have a coffee, please?"

### \[A1\_POD02\] Morning Alarm & Daily Routines (Sabah Rutinleri & Saatler)

- **Süre:** 2:10 Dk (130 sn)  
- **Karakterler:** Sarah (Tasarımcı / Sabah İnsanı) & David (Yazılımcı) \+ Host (Sunucu)  
- **İçerik:** Sabah kaçta uyanıldığını anlatma, kahvaltı tercihleri (omlet, tost), işe bisikletle/metroyla ulaşım alışkanlıkları ve çalışma saatleri.  
- **Hedef Dil Yapıları:** Geniş Zaman (Present Simple: do/does, \-s takısı), Sıklık Zarfları (always, usually, sometimes), Saat söyleme (six thirty, nine o'clock).  
- **Anahtar Kelimeler:** wake up (uyanmak), get up (yataktan kalkmak), commute (işe gidip gelmek), sharp (tam vaktinde), routine (rutin).

#### Tam Podcast Senaryosu (Spoken Script):

**\[Host Intro\]** Welcome back\! In this episode, Sarah and David discuss their morning routines. Listen for daily action verbs and times\!

**Sarah:** Good morning, David\! You look fresh today. What time do you usually wake up? **David:** Good morning, Sarah\! I always wake up at six thirty, but I get out of bed at seven o'clock. What about you? **Sarah:** I am a morning person\! My alarm rings at six o'clock sharp. I always do ten minutes of yoga. **David:** Wow, that is impressive\! Do you eat breakfast at home? **Sarah:** Yes, I usually have an omelet and orange juice. What do you eat? **David:** I only have black coffee and two slices of toast. I never have a heavy breakfast. **Sarah:** How do you commute to the office? **David:** I take the subway. It takes twenty minutes. Do you ride your bike? **Sarah:** Yes, I ride my bike every day when the weather is nice. My workday starts at nine o'clock. **David:** Mine starts at nine too. Let's start working\!

**\[Host Outro\]** Excellent\! Remember: With 'he' and 'she', we say "He wakes up" and "She rides her bike." What is your morning routine?
